Reference

What the pieces do

Carbonite is evidence, not proof. A marker can show that assignment text reached an AI system; it cannot show who did it, when, or whether your course allowed it — and a student who retypes the assignment leaves no trace at all. Tell students in the syllabus that assignments are marked, and follow your institution's process rather than this page.
